## Wording notes
- Mortality and dementia results are from observational studies, so the script says "linked to" or
  "lower risk … during the study", never "prevents".
- "About 80%" is the share of sugar cleared from the blood into muscle measured during insulin-clamp
  studies, which is how the script frames it ("when researchers measured this").

## Cut (no solid primary source found)
- "Walking boosts creativity by 60%" — widely repeated, not in the paper (81% of participants improved is).
- "Two to five minutes of walking after meals lowers blood sugar" (Buffey 2022) — only news coverage found.
- "Up to 22 hours" of lower blood pressure after exercise — only from a heat-exposure study.
- "The calf is your second heart" — a metaphor; replaced by the physiology term "calf muscle pump".
- "The kanji 万 looks like a walking man" — popular story, no reliable source.
