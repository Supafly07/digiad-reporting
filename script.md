# Script — "What Happens to Your Body When You Walk 10,000 Steps Every Day for 30 Days"

Format: vertical 9:16 · ~30 seconds · ~71 words · one calm narrator
Stage: **script only** (no pictures, no code yet)

---

## 1. Narration (word for word)

1. This is one step — now times ten thousand.
2. Each step squeezes the big muscles in your legs.
3. Those working muscles pull sugar out of your blood.
4. So a walk after meals helps steady your blood sugar.
5. Repeat it daily, and your heart grows stronger.
6. Every walk also gives your mood a gentle lift.
7. After thirty days, these small changes add up.
8. Then you lace up, and take the first step again.

---

## 2. Shot list

One continuous camera journey — no hard cuts. We start on a shoe, push down through the leg into the blood, travel up to the heart and brain, then pull back to the whole walker and loop to the shoe.

| # | Sentence | On screen | Camera |
|---|----------|-----------|--------|
| 1 | This is one step — now times ten thousand. | A single walking shoe pressing down. Animation adds the number **10,000** as it counts up. | Slow push-in onto the shoe as it presses the ground. |
| 2 | Each step squeezes the big muscles in your legs. | The shoe becomes the foot of a pair of legs mid-stride. | Keep pushing in; track the stride upward toward the thigh. |
| 3 | Those working muscles pull sugar out of your blood. | A thigh muscle, as if seen under the skin. | Push deeper until the muscle fibers fill the frame. |
| 4 | So a walk after meals helps steady your blood sugar. | A segment of a blood vessel. Animation adds soft-blue sugar dots that drift and get pulled toward the muscle. | Slip from the muscle into the vessel; glide along the flow. |
| 5 | Repeat it daily, and your heart grows stronger. | A human heart. Animation adds a soft-blue pulse with each beat. | Travel up the vessel until the heart fills the frame; steady, calm beat. |
| 6 | Every walk also gives your mood a gentle lift. | A human brain. Animation adds a soft-blue glow spreading outward. | Rise from the heart up to the brain; hold gently. |
| 7 | After thirty days, these small changes add up. | A full figure walking in profile. Animation adds a soft-blue trail of footsteps and the label **Day 30**. | Pull back to reveal the whole walker. |
| 8 | Then you lace up, and take the first step again. | The walker's front foot becomes the single shoe again (loop to shot 1). | Keep pulling back / match-cut the foot to the opening shoe, looping to the start. |
